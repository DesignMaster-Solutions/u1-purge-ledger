On the U1 each toolhead has its own nozzle, so the tower only primes the incoming head: every change costs about the prime_volume of the profile (45 mm3 by default), not the flush matrix. The sparse layers line is the tower printed on layers with no change; wipe_tower_no_sparse_layers = 1 in Snapmaker Orca removes it, and the ledger tells you from one file whether that setting will buy anything. The total is checked against the slicer's own total filament used [g] trailer.
